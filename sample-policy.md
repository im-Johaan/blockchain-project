# Apex Global Corporation: Strategic Merger & Acquisition Policy (Confidential)

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This confidential corporate policy sets forth the mandatory operational guidelines and cryptographic access protocols for all executive committee personnel handling acquisition evaluations, financial audits, and intellectual property transfers.

## 2. Non-Disclosure & Information Security Controls
All proprietary financial statements, corporate valuations, and strategic partner negotiations are designated strictly confidential. Access to these materials is governed directly by decentralized smart contracts. 
Every individual copy issued to personnel is cryptographically marked with zero-width forensic steganography tokens linked to on-chain commitments. Unauthorized duplication, dissemination, or leaking of these materials constitutes a severe regulatory and legal violation.

## 3. Revocation and Expiration Procedures
Corporate access permissions are dynamic. Upon termination of engagement or project reassignment, access rights will be revoked on the DataLeakLedger smart contract. Subsequent download requests will be rejected by the consensus node.
